Family history

For years, all the family knew was that my great-grandfather Fred came from "Vilna." He turned out to be Фаддей Родзевич, born in 1883 in a village near Molodechno, in what is now Belarus. Getting him from there to Greenfield, Massachusetts took two crossings, a decade apart from his wife, and a detour through Buenos Aires.

1883

Born near Molodechno

One of at least five sons of Martin Rodzevich and Elena Kozel.

1905

First trip to America

Arrives in New York on the Kroonland, headed for Greenfield.

1920

Buenos Aires

Sails alone on the SS Vestris to meet Anna, who had made her way there from Vilna.

1921

Ellis Island, together

Fred and Anna arrive on the SS Huron and settle in Greenfield.

Baltimore

Stony Run, old neighborhoods, and the vines that try to eat them.

  • Weed Warriors

    I'm a Baltimore Weed Warrior, pulling English ivy, porcelain berry and multiflora rose out of city parks — almost always with Friends of Stony Run. Muddy work, and you can see the results in an afternoon.

  • Stony Run

    The stream runs past Roland Park, one of the country's early planned suburbs, along the old Ma & Pa railroad route. I'm slowly collecting its history.

About

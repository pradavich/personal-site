About

I'm a genealogy researcher focused on Eastern European Catholic and Orthodox records, emigration through Hamburg and Buenos Aires, and the paper trail families left on both sides of the Atlantic. I like the slow work: reading Cyrillic and Latin scans line by line until a name finally turns up.
